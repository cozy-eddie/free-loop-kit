What Is a Sample in Music?

A sample is a short piece of audio that is taken from an existing recording or newly recorded sound. Producers use samples as building blocks to create beats, melodies, and full songs.

Examples of Samples

  • A single kick drum hit
  • A snare sound
  • A short vocal phrase
  • A melody snippet
  • A sound effect (riser, impact, etc.)

Sample vs Loop

A sample is usually a single sound or short clip.
A loop is a repeating section of music (often made from multiple samples).

Most free sample packs and loop kits are full of these sounds so producers can drag and drop them into their projects.
