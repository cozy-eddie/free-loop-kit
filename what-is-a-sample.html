  <h1>What Is a Sample in Music?</h1>


  <img src="what-is-a-sample.jpg" alt="What is a sample in music – example of a drum sample and melody sample">


  <p>

    A <strong>sample</strong> is a short piece of audio that is taken from an existing recording or newly recorded sound. 

    Producers use samples as building blocks to create beats, melodies, and full songs.

  </p>


  <h2>Examples of Samples</h2>

  <ul>

    <li>A single kick drum hit</li>

    <li>A snare sound</li>

    <li>A short vocal phrase</li>

    <li>A melody snippet</li>

    <li>A sound effect (riser, impact, etc.)</li>

  </ul>


  <h2>Sample vs Loop</h2>

  <p>

    A <strong>sample</strong> is usually a single sound or short clip.<br>

    A <strong>loop</strong> is a repeating section of music (often made from multiple samples).

  </p>


  <p>

    Most free sample packs and loop kits are full of these sounds so producers can drag and drop them into their projects.

  </p>
