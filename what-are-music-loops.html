  <h1>What Are Music Loops?</h1>


  <img src="music-loops-example.jpg" alt="What are music loops – example of a repeating drum and melody loop">


  <p>

    A <strong>music loop</strong> is a short section of audio that repeats seamlessly. 

    It can be a drum beat, a melody, a bassline, or any musical phrase that plays over and over without gaps.

  </p>


  <h2>How Music Loops Work</h2>

  <p>

    Producers create or download loops and place them in their DAW (like FL Studio or Ableton). 

    Because the loop is designed to repeat perfectly, it creates a continuous rhythm or melody with almost no effort.

  </p>


  <h2>Common Types of Music Loops</h2>

  <ul>

    <li>Drum loops</li>

    <li>Melody loops</li>

    <li>Bass loops</li>

    <li>Vocal loops</li>

    <li>FX loops</li>

  </ul>


  <p>

    Music loops are one of the fastest ways to start making beats, especially for beginners.

  </p>
