What Are Music Loops?

A music loop is a short section of audio that repeats seamlessly. It can be a drum beat, a melody, a bassline, or any musical phrase that plays over and over without gaps.

How Music Loops Work

Producers create or download loops and place them in their DAW (like FL Studio or Ableton). Because the loop is designed to repeat perfectly, it creates a continuous rhythm or melody with almost no effort.

Common Types of Music Loops

  • Drum loops
  • Melody loops
  • Bass loops
  • Vocal loops
  • FX loops

Music loops are one of the fastest ways to start making beats, especially for beginners.
